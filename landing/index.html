Under the hood

How it works

STEP 01
News scraping
Every hour, our pipeline scrapes the latest crypto headlines across BTC, ETH, SOL, XRP and 5 more assets.
STEP 02
FinBERT scoring
Each headline passes through FinBERT — returning a sentiment score from −1 (bearish) to +1 (bullish).
STEP 03
Correlation analysis
We calculate the lag between sentiment and price movement, finding the optimal predictive window per asset.
STEP 04
Signal delivery
Signals surface in the dashboard and via API. Traders get insight. Developers get data. Both get an edge.
