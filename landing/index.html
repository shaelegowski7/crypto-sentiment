A note on signal strength
Crypto markets are noisy. We don't claim sentiment predicts price with certainty — no tool does. What SentimentFX gives you is quantified context: how the market narrative is shifting, scored objectively, updated hourly. Use it to inform your thinking, not replace it. Our correlation engine tells you honestly whether a signal is strong, weak, or inconclusive — with p-values and confidence intervals, not marketing copy.
Under the hood

How it works

STEP 01
News scraping
Every hour, our pipeline scrapes the latest headlines across BTC, ETH, SOL, XRP, DOGE, EUR/USD, GBP/USD and USD/JPY.
STEP 02
FinBERT scoring
Each headline passes through FinBERT — returning a sentiment score from −1 (bearish) to +1 (bullish).
STEP 03
Correlation analysis
We measure whether sentiment shifts historically correlate with next-day returns, with honest signal strength ratings and confidence intervals.
STEP 04
Signal delivery
Signals surface in the dashboard and via API. Traders get context. Developers get structured data. No black boxes.
