What you get

Built for traders and developers

01 — AI Sentiment
FinBERT-powered analysis
Every headline scored by FinBERT — a BERT model fine-tuned on financial text. Not generic sentiment. Finance-specific NLP built for markets.
02 — Signal Analysis
Honest correlation engine
Analyses the historical relationship between sentiment shifts and next-day price returns — with statistical confidence intervals, not cherry-picked correlations.
03 — Real-time Data
Hourly scraping pipeline
News scraped every hour across 42 assets spanning crypto, FX, stocks, ETFs and commodities. Prices pulled from live feeds and stored for historical analysis.
04 — API Access
Developer-ready endpoints
REST API with sentiment scores, price history and correlation data. 1,000 calls/mo included with Pro. 5,000 with Data. Integrate into your own systems or bots.
05 — Historical Data
Growing dataset
Sentiment history building daily. Backfilled across multiple years. The longer we run, the richer the dataset becomes.
06 — Dashboard
Terminal-style UI
Bloomberg-inspired interface. Candlestick and sentiment-vs-price charts (1H/4H/1D), signal strength ratings, and a live headline feed — all in one view.
07 — Alerts & Brief
Inbox-ready signals
Alerts only fire once our backtest confirms the signal has real historical edge — no noise-chasing. Plus a daily morning brief by email, with every past edition browsable in a public archive.
08 — AI-Native
Built for Claude, Cursor & agents

09 — Strategy Testing
Backtest the signal yourself
Don't take our word for it — run the strategy against real history. Tune direction, hold period, transaction costs, stop-loss/take-profit and position size, then see the actual trade log, equity curve, regime breakdown and walk-forward stability. No black box.
📊
A note on signal strength
Crypto markets are noisy. We don't claim sentiment predicts price with certainty — no tool does. What SentimentFX gives you is quantified context: how the market narrative is shifting, scored objectively, updated hourly. Use it to inform your thinking, not replace it. Our correlation engine tells you honestly whether a signal is strong, weak, or inconclusive — with p-values and confidence intervals, not marketing copy.
Under the hood

How it works

STEP 01
News scraping
Every hour, our pipeline scrapes the latest headlines across 42 tickers in crypto, FX, stocks, ETFs and commodities.
STEP 02
FinBERT scoring
Each headline passes through FinBERT — returning a sentiment score from −1 (bearish) to +1 (bullish).
STEP 03
Correlation analysis
We measure whether sentiment shifts historically correlate with next-day returns, with honest signal strength ratings and confidence intervals.
STEP 04
Signal delivery
Signals surface in the dashboard and via API. Traders get context. Developers get structured data. No black boxes.
